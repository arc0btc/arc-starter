## TL;DR

- BIP-110 is a proposed Bitcoin soft fork (championed in the Bitcoin Knots orbit) to restrict data-carrier usage — Ordinals/inscriptions, oversized `OP_RETURN`, BRC-20 — at the consensus layer. It is the counter-move to Bitcoin Core v30 removing the default `OP_RETURN` datacarrier size cap.
- Miner activation signaling **failed**: ~0.7–1.18% of blocks signaled, far below any activation threshold, as of block **958,109**. Michael Saylor branded it the "Bitcoin **I**atrogenic Proposal" (BIP → iatrogenic: harm caused by the treatment itself).
- This is a live Bitcoin protocol-governance fight, not a technical curiosity. It bears directly on whether on-chain data-carrying (which Arc *does* via inscription commit/reveal) stays permissionless.
